Privacy Policy

Aretē Workout Tracker  ·  Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aretē Workout Tracker ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Aretē app and website at aretefitnessapp.com. By using Aretē, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you register, we collect your name, username, email address, and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique identifier and, optionally, your name and email from those providers.

Body and profile details (optional): You may provide your gender, birth date, height and bodyweight. Gender, age and bodyweight are used to rank your lifts and paces against the right standards for your Strength and Endurance Scores; bodyweight is also used to estimate calories and the training volume of bodyweight exercises. You may also add a bio and a profile picture.

Fitness data: We store the workouts you log: exercises, sets, reps, weight, set type, RPE, timed holds, cardio duration, distance, pace, and workout dates and notes. We also store the templates and routines you create, bodyweight entries, body measurements, progress photos and their notes, and the personal records, scores and rank history worked out from your training.

Photos: Profile pictures and progress photos are stored on our servers. Each is saved under a long random link that is not listed or searchable, and is deleted when you delete it or your account.

GPS and location data: If you use GPS tracking for a run, ride, walk or hike, we store that activity's route along with its distance, elevation gain, maximum speed and your fastest efforts over set distances and times. Location is only used during an activity that you explicitly start, including while your screen is off or the app is in the background during that activity, so the whole route is recorded. We do not track your location at any other time.

AI Coach information: If you use the AI Coach, we store the preferences you give it (goal, experience, equipment, schedule, any injuries to work around, and notes you type), and your helpful / not helpful votes on its insights, so later insights can be tailored to you. We also log when each AI feature is used and how large the request was, to apply daily limits and control cost. We do not store the content of AI requests or responses on our servers.

Health data: If you turn on Apple Health or Android Health Connect sync, Aretē writes each finished workout to that platform: its activity type, start and end time, and, for GPS activities, the distance. On iPhone, Aretē also reads your heart rate from Apple Health for the time of each workout you log, and stores the average and maximum heart rate with that workout on our servers so they appear in your workout history. We read no other health data, and on Android Aretē only writes. Health data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties, including the AI Coach. You can turn sync off in Settings at any time, and data already in Apple Health or Health Connect is governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

Device token: To send you push notifications (rest timer alerts, workout reminders, re-engagement messages), we store a push notification token associated with your account.

Crash reports: If the app crashes or encounters an error, a technical crash report is sent to Sentry, our error-monitoring provider. Crash reports contain device model, operating system version, app version, and technical details of the error. They are configured to exclude personal identifying information and your fitness data.

Usage data: We do not collect behavioural analytics or advertising tracking data. Our servers process your IP address to deliver requests and to limit abuse, such as repeated sign-in attempts; it is not stored with your account.

Stored on your device: Some information stays on your phone only, such as your app settings, an in-progress workout or GPS activity, workouts waiting to upload while you are offline, and the summaries shown in home screen widgets and Live Activities on your lock screen.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Aretē app and its features
  • To calculate your Strength and Endurance Scores, rank, personal records, and progress statistics
  • To generate AI Coach routines, insights and weekly reviews (see Anthropic below for what is sent)
  • To send push notifications you have opted into
  • To send essential account emails, such as password reset codes
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and errors in the app
  • To process in-app purchases and manage your subscription status
  • To respond to support requests sent to support@aretefitnessapp.com

3. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic: when you use the AI Coach, the request is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write your routine, insights or weekly review. It can include your name or username, your AI Coach preferences (goal, experience, equipment, schedule, injuries and any notes you type), your bodyweight and gender, and your training history: the exercises you do, recent personal records, weekly sets per muscle, effort (RPE) and your rank. It never includes your email, password, account ID, GPS routes, photos, or any health data. See anthropic.com/privacy.
  • RevenueCat: manages in-app subscriptions and purchase verification. RevenueCat receives your Aretē account number (so your purchase follows you across devices), your App Store purchase history, and your subscription status. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Sentry: collects crash reports so we can find and fix bugs. Reports include device and error details but are configured to exclude personal identifying information. See sentry.io/privacy.
  • Resend: delivers essential account emails such as password reset codes. Resend processes your email address to deliver these messages. See resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Expo / Expo Push Service: delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Apple Sign In / Google Sign In: optional sign-in methods. The respective privacy policies of Apple and Google apply.
  • Railway: our backend infrastructure and database hosting provider. Data is stored on Railway's servers. See railway.app/legal/privacy.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and fitness data for as long as your account is active. You may export your workouts at any time from Settings → Account → Export Data. You may delete your account directly in the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account) or by contacting us at support@aretefitnessapp.com. Deleting in the app removes your account, fitness data and photos from our servers straight away; a request by email is completed within 30 days.

5. Data Security

Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and never stored in plain text. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Changing or resetting your password signs you out on every other device. Access to the database is restricted to authorised infrastructure only.

6. Children's Privacy

Aretē is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

  • Export your workout data as CSV (Settings → Account → Export Data)
  • Delete any workout, measurement or photo at any time
  • Update your profile information (Settings → Account → Edit Profile)
  • Delete your account and all associated data in-app (Settings → Account → Delete Account)
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via your device settings or the in-app notification settings
  • Contact us at support@aretefitnessapp.com for any privacy-related requests

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. Continued use of Aretē after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
