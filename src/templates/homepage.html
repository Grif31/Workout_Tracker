Home

Your week, the moment you open it

A running workout streak, this week at a glance, and your active routine ready to log. Recent sessions list their exercises, reps and volume, with every personal record you hit that day.

Plans

Your next workout, ready to start

Follow a routine and Aretē knows which day is up next, even when life moves your schedule around. See where you are in the rotation and what's on the day, then start it in one tap. Your templates sit right below, ready to log.

Strength Score

See exactly where you stand

Your lifts are scored against real strength standards for your bodyweight, so you get a percentile instead of a guess. A full-body map ranks every muscle group, showing what is carrying you and what is lagging.

Progress

Your progress, at a glance

See which days you trained against a weekly goal you set yourself, your Strength and Endurance Scores side by side, and volume, sets, workouts or distance compared with the four weeks before. Below, each muscle group is measured against MEV, MAV and MRV landmarks so you know what is below target and what is on track.

Weekly Summary

Workouts against your goal, total volume and reps, training time and your streak, with how each compares to your average. See which days you trained, which muscles got the work, and your most improved lift.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aretē? + −
Aretē is a workout tracking app for iOS focused on helping you measure and pursue excellence in your training. Log strength and cardio workouts, track personal records, monitor your progress over time, and get AI-generated programs tailored to your goals.
Is Aretē free? + −
The core workout logging features are free. Aretē Premium unlocks the AI Coach, AI-generated workout templates and routines, and additional customisation options. Premium is available as a monthly subscription, annual subscription, or one-time lifetime purchase.
What is the Greek Rank system? + −
Your Greek Rank is a score from 0 to 100 that reflects your overall training excellence across consistency, strength, volume, and dedication. Ranks progress from Neophyte through Athlete, Hero, Demigod, Olympian, Titan, and finally Aretē, the pinnacle of training achievement.
How does the AI Coach work? + −
The AI Coach (Premium) analyses your recent training history, personal records, and goals to generate personalised workout templates and full weekly routines. You can configure your goal, experience level, available equipment, and session length to get programs that fit your life.
Is my workout data secure? + −
Yes. All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties beyond what is necessary to operate the app (see our Privacy Policy for full details).
How do I cancel my subscription? + −
Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store. Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone to cancel at any time. Your Premium access continues until the end of the current billing period.
How do I delete my account? + −
You can delete your account from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account) or by emailing support@aretefitnessapp.com. All your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
